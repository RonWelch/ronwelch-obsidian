The 10-year yield hit 5.04%, and the 30-year hit 5.36% yesterday, in part due to inflation fears as the Iran war sends oil prices above $106 per barrel. The 10-year yield is often used as a benchmark for borrowing costs, so its rise may drive up mortgage rates. Higher-yielding bonds could also spur investors to shift money from stocks to bonds, pushing the stock market down. US stock markets closed down on the news (S&P 500 -0.5%, Dow -0.6%, Nasdaq -0.8%).
