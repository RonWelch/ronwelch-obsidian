holding your money is a very effective way for a bank to keep selling you things

stablecoins and self custodial accounts provide the necessary decoupling and let the money live in an account you control. financial providers plug into it to do a job

think openrouter, but for financial services

one provider has the best FX quote, another can settle faster, another has the local payment rail you need, use whoever does the job best

switching providers should be a routing decision instead of moving your entire financial life to another bank

we're not quite there yet. but the direction is clear. the growth of stablecoin orchestration ecosystem is not slowing down. there is fierce competition on fx, rails and scalable approach to compliance

having to earn the next transaction because holding the customer's money no longer guarantees you get it will naturally lead to more efficiency and a win for the customer
