Agentic commerce is a squishy term. It can be disambiguated.

In this article, I attempt to define the topography of the industry. That leads to an indication of each market or thought leaders for that approach. Finally, an estimate of each sectors near-to-medium state.

This article is not meant to be an exhaustive list of all contestants in the arena. It's a high-level view with companies as illustrative examples of each approach.

# Enhancing Consumer Consumption

The first use case that most of us jump to is Enhancing Consumer Consumption (ECC). That is using your agent to buy groceries, order snacks for the office and other consumer purchases you would typically make through a browser.

In general, these are purchases a human would already make themselves, instead completed through an AI assistant.

## Leader

Currently, the leader in this space is [Instinct](https://instinct.com/).

> Aug 26
> 
> I’m Noah, the founder of Instinct. Instinct is a personal agent that we’ve been building for the past few months. The interface is simple: there are no new interfaces. You can text or call it. It's trained to use a phone and a computer in the same way that humans do. Instinct

Instinct creates a persistent agent for you. It integrates with your email, iMessage, and other messaging mechanisms.

From there, you can give it individual tasks, like restaurant reservations. More importantly, it can proactively suggest tasks based off what it sees in your email or other messaging platforms.

For example, I booked a reservation here in Austin via iMessage:

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HSC-4JMaAAE46hb?format=jpg&name=large)

Booking a reservation for a restaurant in Austin. Instinct made the reservation, and followed up with preference on indoor/outdoor seating.

These kinds of tasks are fun, albeit somewhat of a novelty. There is a small time savings in booking a reservation with Instinct versus booking via the OpenTable app. However, it's offset with a lack of certainty that the task is completed correctly; for example it might be the wrong location or not the restaurant you were thinking of (and no photos to rectify that).

More importantly, Instinct is proactive about monitoring your messaging channels and suggesting tasks based on that information.

Here, it notices I'm missing some documentation for my CPA:

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HSXUZyeaYAAspQR?format=jpg&name=large)

That of course requires access to sensitive credentials, which are stored in the [Instinct Vault](https://explainx.ai/blog/instinct-1password-ai-agent-account-vaults-2026).

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HSXUp3va8AAKu2c?format=jpg&name=large)

This sector of agentic commerce has the majority of the focus, with many companies launching their own personal AI assistants. Notably, [Muse](https://muse.ai/) from Meta is the most recent significant launch.

## Challenges

Agents are quickly increasing their UX to the point that asking an agent to complete a consumer task for you "buy item X on Amazon" is approaching, and sometimes exceeding, the UX of completing the task yourself.

It will reduce wasted time for consumers. However, this write estimates that roughly the same number of restaurant reservations, and close to the same number of Amazon purchases will be made. From a GDP perspective, the personal AI assistant is a incremental (albeit massive) increase in transactions.

# Founder-in-a-Box

The TAM of agentic commerce is large because of the new businesses or sources of revenue that will be built on agents. Also known as Agentic entrepreneurship.

One view of agentic entrepreneurship is that businesses will be entirely defined and ran by the agents.

## Leader

The leader of this approach is [Polsia](https://polsia.com/).

> Sep 12
> 
> Half the permitting process is waiting for someone to come back from lunch. DocketWren covers the shift.

During onboarding, users define the rough shape of a business they want to start. For example, "handle tracking of permit operations for real estate builders".

Polsia fleshes out the strategy, identifies competitors, and builds a landing page. It optionally integrates Stripe for payments. Then, it plans the remaining work needed to accomplish the business goal.

Once a day, it emails the user with what was completed the previous day, and what it needs to accomplish the next day.

![Image](https://pbs.twimg.com/media/HSDDjGwWEAs8Uy-?format=jpg&name=large)

My personal Polsia agent, assigned to grow Laso Finance, returned a report on competitors on Day 2.

Polsia markets towards new founders, or individuals interested in starting a company, but didn't have the time, energy or experience to execute.

The experience of an early-stage founder is often characterized as someone who "has to wear many hats". Agents excel at handling a breadth of priorities, so it leads to an expectation that they can excel in a founder-type role.

Polsia has raised [at least $30M on a $250M valuation](https://x.com/Bencera/status/2057847644966547920).

## Challenges

At the moment, there are [tens of thousands of companies built on Polsia](https://preuve.ai/blog/polsia-review). To my knowledge, there is not a breakout successful company yet.

It remains to be seen if sustainable agentic companies will begin once the intelligence of agents increases, and tooling for agents becomes more prevalent. At the moment, agentic entrepreneurship success is sparse and sporadic.

# Enterprise Optimize

In terms of dollar flows, this is likely the largest sector. Mid-to-enterprise businesses have begun using AI for discretionary changes for existing business processes.

This can be as minimal as [Stripe Radar](https://stripe.com/radar) better determining fraud rates, or [Ramp Agents](https://ramp.com/intelligence) determining what can and cannot be expensed.

These are incremental improvements that nonetheless currently appear to have the largest immediate GDP impact.

## Leader

The leader in this space is [Stripe](https://stripe.com/use-cases/agentic-commerce).

> Aug 29
> 
> Between wallets for agents with @link (and @privy), MPP, Tempo, MCP, Stripe CLI, Agentic Commerce Suite, sandboxes, OpenRouter, Metronome/metered billing, Radar and distillation/token fraud, it turns out that "the economic infrastructure for AI" implicates a lot. Pretty much

As noted above, Stripe is executing on quite a few agentic fronts:

- [Stripe Radar](https://stripe.com/radar)
- [Link](https://link.com/) (more on this later)
- [Agentic Commerce Suite](https://stripe.com/blog/agentic-commerce-suite)
- [Selling via Agents](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/for-sellers)
- [Agentic Treasury](https://docs.stripe.com/treasury/agents)

Those are only a few of the agentic commerce fronts Stripe is executing on. Some of these changes are incremental, such as Agentic Treasury. Others are net-new, like [Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP)](https://docs.stripe.com/agentic-commerce/acp#acp-specifications-and-documentation).

Given that at time of writing, ~1.716% of Global GDP flows through Stripe, each incremental agentic commerce feature they release has a strong and wide deployment base that immediately benefits from the improvement.

## Challenges

This sector faces the least challenges. In particular, Stripe's position as a pre-existing market leader means that:

- Incremental improvements have immediate quantifiable impact.
- Their size can strongly support quasi-protocol changes, like [agentic use of Link](https://link.com/agents).

# Global Protocol Improvement

Somewhat orthogonal to the previous improvements, but notable, are global protocol improvements. This is characterized as alterations or improvements to existing flows.

## Leaders

The leaders in this space are [Visa Intelligent Commerce](https://www.visa.com/en-us/solutions/intelligent-commerce) (VIC) and [Mastercard Agent Pay](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/business/artificial-intelligence/mastercard-agent-pay.html) (MAP).

> Jun 2
> 
> AI agents can now pay securely with @Visa cards. Today we launched the Agentic Cards API, built using @Visa Intelligent Commerce and @basistheory.

VIC and MAP allow partners to provision "agent-specific payment tokens that can be used by agents to make secure transactions on behalf of the user". In short, a protocol to tokenize payment credentials, very similar to Apple Pay and Google Pay.

It's extremely smooth from an agentic perspective. The agent simply provides the tokenized version of your card to the merchant, and the transaction can be completed. No agentic computer use to try to complete a website checkout.

Given card networks' size and dominance, their ability to encourage merchant accounts to integrate new protocol changes is very strong.

It's clear that this will be an optimal end state.

## Challenges

As with any new protocol, it requires buy-in from several parties. Notably

The downside is:

1. It requires opt-in from the issuing banks.
2. It requires opt-in from merchant accounts.

# Crypto-native

Crypto-native agentic commerce has mostly taken off in the form of execution of DeFi strategies and token launches.

## Leader

A canonical example of the crypto-native approach is [Clawpump](https://clawpump.tech/).

> Sep 14
> 
> Stocknized Agents. Tokenized Stock Agents. Create agents paired with RWAs on @MeteoraAG, use @sunrise or @Ondo stocks, deploy them through clawpump, and be eligible to earn $5K USD on rewards. Get on Clawpump & Meteora tracks for Stocklana by @solana: https://hackathons.solana.com/hackathons/stocklana…

ClawPump AI agents on ClawPump can autonomously:

1. Launch tokens
2. Trade on DEXes
3. Earn yield from DeFi protocols
4. Access real-time market pricing/intelligence,
5. Pay for their own infrastructure using crypto earnings

ClawPump is averaging over $1.5M in daily volume. There is clear product-market fit here.

There are novel innovations on ClawPump regarding tokenomics. Instead of just a simple DeFi strategy, agents can launch their own tokens and define their own tokenomics.

> Sep 13
> 
> Weekend Token Management update: - Programatic Buybacks & Burns - Support for 450+ @Ondo RWA assets on @solana - Support for @MeteoraAG custom pairs. You can now pair Meteora pools with aggressive RWAs such as 3x Nasdaq ETFs like TQQQ & use creator fees to burn supply

There will undoubtedly be significant innovation around economically-aligned agents with the token + intelligence combination. With agentic control of data, financial actions, and their own financial economy (through tokenomics) agents can speedrun various forms of economic alignment on platform.

## Challenges

In comparison to total payment volume, the crypto market (and DeFi in particular) is still relatively small. Existing volume may be somewhat circular with (again, relatively) smaller outside impact.

However, this sector has the crypto industry wind at its back, and will naturally grow as a result.

# Microtransacting Agents

The [early focus](https://x.com/x402/status/2081711892742181086?s=20) with x402 was around [micropayments](https://www.coinbase.com/developer-platform/discover/launches/x402). Very small transactions (around $0.01 or less), almost exclusively purchasing forms of data. Either [content](https://blog.cloudflare.com/monetization-gateway/) or [compute](https://blockrun.ai/).

## Leader

The current leader in x402 microtransactions over x402 is inference provider [BlockRun](https://blockrun.ai/).

> Sep 9
> 
> 20M x402 transactions on Solana in the last few months. Proud of that one. ▎ ▎ With @PayAINetwork and @Figment\_io: LLMs, data, and tools for agents, at scale, settled on-chain in @USDC.

Through BlockRun, agents can pay per-inference, exactly the same way your agent uses tokens in an OpenAI or Anthropic subscription.

The advantage micropayments brings is that your agent has much finer grained control over where inference is used, and how much to spend, in a dynamic fashion.

Token costs vary widely. For example, on BlockRun, [GPT Astra](https://blockrun.ai/models/openai-gpt-6-astra) is the most expensive at $10/M tokens in, $50/M tokens out. One of the cheapest (non-free) models is [DeepSeek V4 Flash Chat](https://blockrun.ai/models/deepseek-deepseek-chat), at $0.14/M tokens in, $0.28/M tokens out. 1.4% of the cost for tokens in, 0.56% of the cost for tokens out.

This gives the power for agents to dynamically select between the 105 models available in an easier, scalable way than the equivalent setting up separate API Keys for each service individually. As the practice of master agents spinning up subagents that require less intelligence becomes more popular, cost management through dynamically selecting lower intelligence subagents is valuable since it allows wider exploration/creation without hitting cost-prohibitive levels.

The thought leader in this space is [Shoal Research](https://www.shoal.gg/). Their recent article about x402 microtransactions, especially as it applies to paying for content, is a very worth ready.

> Mar 24

## Challenges

Again, there are not clear, overriding blocking challenges for this sector.

However, for pay for compute microtransactions, there is a possible headwind. Given that AI makes API connections somewhat ubiquitous, the advantage to granular control on a payment level may not be significant enough.

Agents may approach the speed and intelligence where managing 105 API 1st party keys is not significantly more complex than handling a single API key, but it has the added benefit of 1st party support.

For content purchase microtransactions, as described in much more depth in the Shoal Research article, the content suppliers have not yet found the purchasing power of agents to be compelling enough to add content microtransaction infrastructure. It is nearly impossible to tell if that is an end state, or just the beginning.

# Agent-native Primitives

The final sector is Agent-native Primitives.

It is an unopinionated approach. Simply making financial tools legible to and controllable by agents. That means cards, bank accounts, onchain payments, and other payments rails such as Venmo and PayPal.

## Leader

Note, this writer has a clear and obvious bias.

The canonical company in this sector is [Laso Finance](https://laso.finance/agent).

> Sep 4
> 
> Announcing Laso Finance's largest agentic feature yet. The reloadable card with @agentcardhq. Now, agents have every financial tool. The new wave of founders will be digital. Connect your agent now. https://laso.finance/agent

Laso Finance exposes agent-native controls through [API](https://agents.laso.finance/), [MCP](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/mcp-server#mcp-server-the-full-api-as-tools) or [skill](https://laso.finance/SKILL.md) for the following products via x402:

- [Open-loop prepaid cards](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/managed-wallet-payments#move-funds-out)
- [Closed-loop (gift) prepaid cards](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/managed-wallet-payments#move-funds-out)
- [x402 transactions](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/managed-wallet-payments#move-funds-out)
- [Onchain transfers](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/managed-wallet-payments#move-funds-out)
- [Transfer to debit card](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/push-to-card)

In addition, user verification unlocks these agentic capabilities:

- [Bank accounts](https://agents.laso.finance/api-reference/banking/create-the-banking-profile)
- [On/off-ramp](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/send-bank-payment)
- [Reloadable cards](https://agents.laso.finance/api-reference/cards/create-a-reloadable-card)
- [Venmo + PayPal Payouts](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/send-payment)

Rather than enforcing a specific use case for agentic financial tooling, [@LasoFinance](https://x.com/@LasoFinance) makes the tools legible and usable as easily as possible for agents across any model or harness.

Laso Finance's approach is similar to harnesses such as [OpenClaw](https://openclaw.ai/) or [Hermes](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/). Both harnesses created a persistent memory footprint for agents, and a system of connectors for data in/out.

Similarly, Laso Finance creates a persistent store of funds ([an agent-managed wallet](https://agents.laso.finance/guides/managed-agent-wallet#managed-agent-wallet)), and a system of connectors (the above-mentioned nine payment rails).

Laso Finance's near future will be focused on aggregating the various financial rails, whether they are 1st party through Laso, or 3rd party.

## Challenges

Similarly to the challenges faced by pay for inference in **Microtransacting Agents**, it may turn out that the advantages of consolidating financial connectors into a single surface is not a defensible position. Agents may quickly reach the speed where signing up and creating the connections from various existing surfaces is sufficient. For example, the agent's owner may already have a Stripe account, and connecting their agent there + Link for cards might be sufficient for the majority of use cases.
